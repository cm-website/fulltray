About Fulltray

Making Food Sharing Simple and Meaningful

Fulltray is an innovative platform designed to connect individuals and organizations with excess food to those in need within their community.

By facilitating the sharing of surplus food, we aim to reduce food waste and foster a sense of community and compassion. Whether you have leftovers from an event, surplus groceries, or simply want to help your neighbors, Fulltray makes it simple.
