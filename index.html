App Description

Fulltray is an innovative platform designed to connect individuals and organizations with excess food to those in need within their community. By facilitating the sharing of surplus food, Fulltray aims to reduce food waste and foster a sense of community and compassion. Whether you have leftovers from an event, surplus groceries, or simply want to help your neighbors, Fulltray makes food sharing simple and meaningful. The app provides an easy-to-use interface for donors to list available food items and for recipients to find and claim them. Join Fulltray today and be a part of the solution to food waste while helping to build a stronger, more connected community.
